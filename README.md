# Cartagena Together

A cozy pixel-art game about a couple exploring Cartagena, Colombia.

## Play
Double-click `game/index.html` (opens in your browser, no internet or installs needed).

Two players on one keyboard:
- **Monty:** W A S D to walk, **E** (or Space) to act
- **Summer:** arrow keys to walk, **Enter** to act
- **P** photo album · **M** map · **N** music on/off · **F** "Follow" (an idle partner walks after the other, for one player)

Each player moves on their own, but they can't wander off-screen from each other.
Walk into the sea to swim, or stand next to a kayak and press your act key to paddle (press it again next to the sand to climb out).
Talk to the locals: dance with Don Rafa, share an arepa or a coconut, ride Don Lucho's horse carriage, go fishing with Don Efraín, say hola to the sea turtle on the island, and more. Press act next to your partner for a hug.

Collect all **15 seashells** and **10 memories** (both of you stand at a ♥ spot to take a photo) to see the ending.
Take the boat from the dock (south of the Clock Tower) to the Islas del Rosario.
Progress saves automatically.
